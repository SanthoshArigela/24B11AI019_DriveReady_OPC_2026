# Company profile

- Working name: Medivora AI
- Proposed legal form: Medivora AI (OPC) Private Limited (not incorporated or cleared)
- Founder: Arigela Tejasri Sai Santhosh
- Proposed year: 2026
- Sector: HealthTech, B2B SaaS, Agentic AI
- Flagship product: Medivora Copilot
- Initial segment: independent pharmacies in India
- Tagline: Intelligent Pharmacy. Better Operations.

## Vision
Make reliable operational intelligence accessible to pharmacies of every size.

## Mission
Build affordable, secure AI tools that help pharmacy staff understand stock, expiry, sales and purchasing records while retaining human control.

## Values
Accuracy, transparency, privacy, affordability, human accountability and measurable impact.

## Positioning
A source-backed operational intelligence layer, initially complementing existing pharmacy systems rather than replacing them. Differentiate through batch-aware alerts, explainable recommendations and staff approval. Competitive advantage is a hypothesis, not a validated claim.

## Brand direction
Deep teal #092E30, mint #5EEAD4 and white. A simple medical cross with a data pulse is a proposed visual identity; name, trademark, domain and logo clearance remain outstanding.
