# Business model

## Customer and value proposition
Buyer: independent pharmacy owner. Users: pharmacists, managers and authorized employees. Offer faster inventory lookup, timely expiry review, simpler reporting and controlled restocking suggestions, without requiring immediate replacement of billing software.

## Business model canvas
| Area | Hypothesis |
|---|---|
| Segments | Independent pharmacies, later small chains |
| Value | Source-backed operations intelligence |
| Channels | Local demos, referrals, pharmacy associations, website |
| Relationships | Guided onboarding, support, feedback |
| Revenue | Subscription per store, optional onboarding and integrations |
| Resources | Engineering, authorized records, hosting, domain expertise |
| Activities | Integration, testing, product support, security |
| Partners | Pharmacists, software vendors, hosting providers |
| Costs | Hosting, inference, storage, support, security, onboarding |

## Illustrative pricing (not validated)
Starter ₹499/month: stock and expiry queries. Professional ₹1,499/month: reporting and draft reorder tools. Multi-store: negotiated pricing. Limit usage transparently based on measured costs. No prices are committed.

## Example unit economics
At 20 stores paying ₹1,499/month, gross monthly subscription revenue would be ₹29,980 before taxes, discounts and churn. If variable costs are ₹400/store/month, contribution is ₹21,980 before fixed costs. These are illustrative assumptions, not forecasts or profit.

## Go-to-market
Interview 5–10 owners; choose one recurring workflow; demo with synthetic data; pilot at one consenting store; measure outcomes; test willingness to pay; expand only when reliable. Avoid unsolicited messaging.

## Risks
Stale records: freshness and fail-closed behavior. Bad AI answers: deterministic queries and citations. Data exposure: minimal collection and access boundaries. Adoption: complement existing systems. Costs: usage budgets and model routing. Regulatory and incorporation requirements: obtain appropriate professional verification before launch.
